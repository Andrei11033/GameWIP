# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our standard

GameWIP welcomes good-faith participation from people of different experience
levels and backgrounds. Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and
code, not people. Assume mistakes can be corrected, and help keep technical
discussion useful to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or repeated personal attacks. Spam, trolling, and knowingly unsafe
or misleading contributions are also unacceptable.

## Project spaces

This standard applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, project
boards, and other spaces where someone is representing GameWIP. Technical
disagreement is expected; hostility and intimidation are not.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
method on the owner's GitHub profile. If no private contact is visible, open an
issue that asks only for a private conduct-reporting channel; do not include the
concern or any sensitive details in that issue. The maintainer may edit or
remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, issue warnings, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
project and its participants.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as practical. Anyone handling
a report must avoid conflicts of interest and unnecessary disclosure.
